# CampusAI - Hackathon Demo Script

## 0:00 - The Problem
"Most campus systems are built around information. CampusAI is built around ACTION. Students, visitors, and staff waste time figuring out *how* to use the campus. We built an intelligent layer on top of OpenStreetMap to solve this."

## 0:30 - The Copilot Experience
- Type in the chat: "Where is the library?"
- Point out that it doesn't just give text, it automatically triggers a route on the map!
- Demonstrate multilingual: "சிஎஸ்இ லேப் எங்கே இருக்கிறது?" (Tamil for Where is the CSE lab?)

## 1:00 - Accessibility Mode
- Enable **♿ Accessible Route**.
- Show how the route dynamically alters to avoid known stairs/steps on the map.
- "We believe smart campuses must be inclusive campuses."

## 1:30 - Overlooked Workforce Mode
- Change role to **Maintenance** or **Security**.
- Click **🚨 EMERGENCY MODE**.
- Watch the system auto-route to the Medical Center.
- "A smart campus isn't just for students. It's for the people who keep it running."

## 2:00 - Conclusion
"CampusAI. One intelligent layer for a more accessible campus."
